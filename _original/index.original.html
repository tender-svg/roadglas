About Us

Who We Are

RoadGlas®, by Industrial Fabrics, Inc., is a family of paving interlayer solutions engineered to seal pavements, delay reflective cracking and extend the service life of asphalt on streets, highways, airports and parking areas.

With decades of engineering expertise and a commitment to innovation, Industrial Fabrics partners with contractors, engineers and project owners to solve complex pavement challenges reliably, cost-effectively and sustainably.
