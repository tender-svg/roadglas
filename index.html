Benefit Areas

Top 10 Benefits of ROADGLAS®

01

Extends Pavement Life

Defers major deterioration for years compared to standard unreinforced overlays, significantly protecting the initial asset investment.

02

Reduces Cracking

Acts as a robust barrier against reflective cracks reappearing from old pavement below. Fewer ongoing surface repairs and reduced commuter complaints.

03

Lowers Lifecycle Costs

Higher initial installation cost is heavily offset by reduced total ownership costs over the lifecycle.

04

Delays Reconstruction

Reinforced overlays extend existing structures’ useful life to postpone reconstruction for years.

05

Heavy Traffic Performance

High tensile strength fiberglass resists fatigue cracking, severe rutting, shoving, and traffic-induced mechanical stress. Ideal for heavy truck routes.

06

Reduces Disruptions

Fewer lane closures, traffic bottlenecks, and business/transit downtime. Lower overall maintenance labor costs and public inconvenience.

07

Maximizes ROI

Achieves far more years of service from the exact same overlay investment, maximizing the value and efficiency of each paving cycle.

08

Sustainable Preservation

Strengthens existing structures from within rather than completely removing and rebuilding them, with significantly faster project completion times.

09

Proven Technology

Successfully deployed for decades across demanding civil applications, giving highway engineers and specifiers confidence in long-term performance.

10

Contractor Advantage

Offers clear market differentiation, enabling contractors to confidently bid longer warranties and reduce costly callbacks from premature cracking.
