Overview

Hybrid fiberglass structure (Placeholder)

The ROADGLAS® Mat integrates fiberglass mesh within a high-performance polyester mat. Its non-woven structure enables asphalt binder to saturate the fabric, helping to seal the pavement and reduce moisture intrusion.

Unlike polypropylene fabrics, fiberglass mats do not shrink or change dimensions when exposed to hot mix asphalt, preventing premature slippage or loss of bond.

Installation

How It's Installed

1

Repair potholes, fill cracks wider than ¼” (6mm) and clean the surface until dust-free.

2

Apply a hot, uniform tack coat to the full width of the mat plus 2-4 inches on each side.

3

Place the mat immediately, free of wrinkles, and roll in with a pneumatic roller.

Installation Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

Is construction traffic allowed on the products during installation?

Necessary construction traffic is permitted. Steel drum rollers are prohibited.

Is public/transportation traffic allowed on the products during installation?

Minimal traffic is allowed; however, product should be covered by asphalt same day to minimize damage to the material.

How many linear lane miles can be installed on a standard 8-hour day?

An Industrial Fabrics, Inc. crew can install up to 4 to 6 miles of ROADGLAS® PM per day.

Can installed material stay uncovered overnight?

It is recommended that asphalt be applied over installed material same day or within 24 hours of installation. If left unpaved, material must be coned off from traffic, or light sanding applied on top before traffic is allowed.

How do I repair damaged material?

Cut out as much damaged material as possible, retack area and install new material overlapping 6-8 inches.

What if material gets rained on?

Cease paving operations, no traffic allowed, wait until material is completely dry and then begin paving.
