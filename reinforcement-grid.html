Overview

A hidden layer of strength

The grid matrix is composed of fiberglass strands coated with an elastomeric polymer. Fiberglass has high tensile strengths and low elongation properties, which is critical given that asphaltic concrete typically fails at low-strain levels.

Installed between the leveling and surface courses, it redirects vertical crack stresses horizontally to dissipate them. RoadGlas® is to Asphalt what Rebar is to Concrete.

Installation

How It's Installed

1

Repair potholes and fill cracks more than ¼” wide. Milled pavement requires a leveling course, cured a minimum of 24 hours.

2

Place self-adhesive grid wrinkle-free with a laydown tractor, overlapping joints 2 in. longitudinal and 6 in. transverse.

3

Spray hot-applied trackless tack on top of the grid and roll with a pneumatic rubber tire roller.
