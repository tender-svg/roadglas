Join Us for Lunch!

Learn about IFI's geosynthetic solutions over a free lunch — and earn Professional Development Hours while you're at it.

What Is Lunch & Learn?

The program is coordinated by an IFI representative. A presentation typically lasts about an hour depending on questions, and lunch is provided by our company.

01

Who Can Participate?

Engineers, designers, and anyone looking to learn more about IFI's geosynthetic products can participate — including anyone who would like to continue their education and earn Professional Development Hours (PDHs).

02

When Can I Schedule an Event?

Programs are typically scheduled over the lunch hour, but exceptions can be made for breakfast or dinner, depending on what works best for your company.

03

How Do I Request One?

Complete and submit the Lunch & Learn Request Form. Once we receive your request, we'll contact you and begin working on securing a speaker.
