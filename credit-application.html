Terms & General Conditions of Sale

  1. Quotations: Price lists and quotations are valid for thirty (30) days and subject to change without notice thereafter. Special-order quotations are firm for acceptance within forty-five (45) days. Accepted Purchase Order pricing is firm for shipment within ninety (90) days, after which unshipped units are subject to invoicing and 5% per month storage charges.
  2. Payment Terms: Purchases other than cash are subject to credit approval. Open account payments are due Net 30 from invoice date. No retainage is allowed. Amounts unpaid after the due date accrue interest at 18% APR, plus collection costs.
  3. Deliveries: Delivery requests should be placed at least 24 working hours in advance. Deliveries are scheduled first-come, first-served.
  4. Taxes: All applicable taxes at time of shipment are paid by Buyer. Buyer is responsible for providing a tax-exemption certificate with its Purchase Order if a project is tax exempt.
  5. Transportation: Transportation costs not included in FOB destination pricing are paid by Buyer. Unloading time is limited to one hour; additional time is billed at $65.00/hour. Buyer or its representative must be present to sign the delivery ticket.
  6. Returns: Returned products are subject to inspection; credit is issued only for standard stock items in resalable condition, less a 25% restocking charge, within ninety (90) days of purchase. No credit is allowed on custom or special-order products.
  7. Estimates: Industrial Fabrics, Inc. assumes no responsibility for the accuracy of Buyer's requirement estimates; Buyer must supply exact order quantities.
  8. Special and Custom Orders: Signed Purchase Orders with exact quantities are required and, once accepted, are not cancelable. Quoted prices are based on estimated quantities; orders for lesser total quantities are subject to price adjustment.
  9. Quality and Claims: Products are guaranteed to conform only to specifications quoted or published at time of shipment. Buyer must inspect products on delivery and note visible damage on the carrier's Bill of Lading. Industrial Fabrics, Inc. must be notified of any dissatisfaction before installation begins; use of the product constitutes acceptance.
  10. Force Majeure: Industrial Fabrics, Inc. is not liable for failure or delay in performance due to causes beyond its control (acts of God, war, strikes, shortages, and similar events), and shall not be liable for incidental or consequential damages under any circumstances.
  11. Preliminary Notices: Buyer agrees to furnish all information Industrial Fabrics, Inc. needs to timely file a Preliminary Notice on any construction project for which it is furnishing labor and/or materials.
